Bridging the gap between Industry Stakeholders

Real Estate sale/purchase and leasing journeys are paper intensive involving multiple touch points and entities relying on each other’s data and documents.

Our Blockchain and Smart Contracts driven platforms stitch these journeys across multiple organizations — leading to paperless experiences for buyers, sellers, tenants, developers, regulatory authorities, and Financial Institutions.

Our Ready Platform — Zenith Escrow

Investors’ interests are being protected through comprehensive Escrow laws globally, yet enforcement through technology remains a challenge.

ITOO's Zenith Escrow provides an end-to-end escrow management platform that onboards all relevant entities across a blockchain-based, smart-contracts-driven digital solution — ensuring investors’ and developers’ interests are protected in compliance with Escrow law.

Land Provenance

Achieve end-to-end provenance and immutable tracking from allocation of land parcels through construction phases and sale, purchase and leasing transactions.

Immutable land records and ownership history stored across regulators, developers, property management companies and Financial Institutions — with smart-contracts-driven digital inter-organization workflows.

Off Plan Property Leasing

An inter-organization blockchain platform used by real estate regulators, developers and financial institutions to digitize off-plan property purchases.

In compliance with regulatory guidelines, the platform captures and governs leasing transactions through smart contracts between all stakeholders.

Tenancy Contracts Automation

A complete digital ecosystem that enables paperless tenancy contracts initiation and execution across multiple entities.

The platform drives transparent inter-organization workflows across land registration authorities, utility companies, property owners and tenants through smart contracts.

Mortgage Chain

Mortgage Chain digitizes and automates the flow and data of mortgage transactions across all touch points.

The entire lifecycle — from application to closure — is recorded and automated through an immutable blockchain-based data store for complete trust, transparency and efficiency.

Disrupting Real Estate Sector through Tokenization

Tokenization of Real-World Assets is disrupting multiple industries including Real Estate. Our next-gen end-to-end Real Estate Tokenization and Investment platform unlocks ownership through fractionalization, making assets accessible to all segments of the community.

We leverage blockchain, web 3.0 and smart contracts to safeguard asset owners and retail investors. Trusted by numerous developers and marketplace operators, our platform is a secure and compliant one-stop fractional ownership marketplace product.

From the times of COVID-19 crisis we have factored Invoice Finance Frauds

In the times of significant upheaval and uncertainty, the mounting pressure on businesses is making fraudulent activity a constant risk for lenders.

Invoice financing is a major area of fraud where scammers submit the same invoice to multiple banks for processing. Despite its commonality, these frauds are often treated as passive events, where a post-mortem identifies missed red flags, leading to unprevented losses and insufficient measures to reduce future occurrences.

This issue stems from the inability to detect duplicate or non-genuine documents, either within a single organization’s past submissions or across multiple organizations presenting the same document in various sectors.

What is haifin

Etisalat and FAB, in collaboration with ITOO Technologies, have created a solution to address the challenges banks encounter in invoice financing by introducing the UAE’s first invoice discounting fraud prevention platform, haifin.

haifin has been designed using the latest emerging and disruptive technologies such as Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, OCR (ABBYY), and BOTS. This industry-wide solution aims to identify duplicate and fraudulent invoices submitted by banks through the Blockchain network.

haifin is a document clearing exchange system where various document types can be scrutinized, validated, and confirmed without forgery, utilizing advanced blockchain technologies to ensure trust, transparency, and privacy among multiple organizations. Additionally, dynamic rule-based models and Machine Learning / Deep Learning techniques are employed to detect fraudulent and duplicate documents.

Blockchain facilitates seamless onboarding of participants while maintaining the confidentiality, immutability, and transparency of organizational data.

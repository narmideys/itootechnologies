ITOO helps regulators, banking federations and fin-tech consortiums launch national platforms that help address key areas of regulation, compliance and standardization.

Financial regulation is often interpreted and implemented inconsistently within the BFSI sector within many jurisdictions.

Our Ground-Breaking National Financial Regulation & Supervision Platforms

National e-KYC Blockchain Platform

A national-scale blockchain-powered eKYC platform that enables secure and standardized customer verification across financial institutions, reducing duplication, improving compliance and accelerating onboarding.

National Banking Artefacts Exchange Platform

A blockchain-based Bank Trust Network that enables secure verification and exchange of financial artifacts between institutions — enhancing transparency, trust and compliance.

Insurance Recovery Management System

InstaChain digitizes insurance recovery and settlement processes at national scale with smart contract-driven, real-time settlement among participating stakeholders.

National Trade Finance Fraud Detection Platform

A trade finance platform designed to detect fraud and enhance transparency in trade transactions through secure verification and monitoring of trade-related documents and activities.

Regulating Financial Institutions to achieve transparency & efficiency

Regulators have tried to enforce compliance and standardization through notifications and a penalty-driven regime. There has been very little use of inclusive technology to achieve national standardization of pressing challenges such as KYC, AML, TF, TBML and other grave issues.

In highly regulated jurisdictions, banks remain conservative for fear of non-compliance — hindering growth potential and overall economic momentum. In low-regulated jurisdictions, regulators often catch up with frauds under pressure from international bodies.

Our national-scale platforms help regulators standardize, regulate and monitor banking relationships in a non-intrusive manner, while giving banks a technology-enabled safety net to grow while mitigating default and fraud risks.

Our Approach

ITOO's approach to national financial regulation and supervision is industry-wide. We strongly feel any measure or improvement in these areas by individual banks in isolation does not suffice.

We feel industry-led and industry-wide technology-led ventures are more apt vehicles to hedge banks’ risks and give regulators insights into critical areas of compliance.

QAFi

FLAGSHIP

Powering Qatar's Fintech Future

QAFi is a registered trademark of iTOO Technologies and stands for Qatar Fintech Products — a suite of secure, innovative, and regulation-ready solutions designed to accelerate Qatar's digital transformation. Aligned with Qatar's financial vision, QAFi empowers banks, insurers, regulators, and enterprises with next-generation fintech capabilities.

From national e-KYC and interbank artefact exchange to insurance reconciliation and real-world asset tokenization, QAFi platforms foster financial inclusion, unlock new revenue streams, and help position Qatar as a global fintech hub.

  • Blockchain trust

    Shared ledgers for consent-based identity, document exchange, and tamper-proof audit trails across financial institutions.

  • Digital identity

    National-scale e-KYC and orchestration that harmonize standards for banks, exchanges, insurers, and fintechs.

  • AI & analytics

    Intelligent insights that improve visibility, compliance, and decision-making across Qatar's evolving financial ecosystem.

  • Product suite

    Consonance, Assure, InstaChain, Fragments, and more — built under the QAFi umbrella for regulators and enterprises.

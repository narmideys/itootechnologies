Disrupting Hospitality Industry With Fragments

Let's Connect

Fragments is designed to revolutionize the way people interact with travel,
allowing them the chance to be part of something bigger than just a hotel stay.

Convo’s Vision is to unlock the future of travel with an innovative opportunity that transcends the conventional hospitality experience.

Our unique tokenization program with ITOO's Real World Asset Tokenization Platform “Fragments” is designed to revolutionize the way people interact with travel, allowing them the chance to be part of something bigger than just a hotel stay.

Combining our skill of finding and managing high return properties, with our knowledge of financial models and our passion for future technology, we have created a unique model that we are excited to launch and grow.

A Proven Track Record in Modern Hospitality

theConvo was established in 2015, they have successfully turned around 11 properties across Europe and the Mediterranean.

Revenues and Operational profits more than doubled each year since 2021 due to expansion, improved sales and operational efficiency.

They have a proven track record in running profitable hospitality businesses with average occupancy rates of 80%. Breakeven sits at 40%.

They serve modern travelers seeking authentic experiences in vibrant, emerging destinations, with an average Booking.com rating of 8.4/10.

What Sets Convo’s Tokenization Program Apart

Convo’s tokenization program combines a diversified European property portfolio with higher-than-average returns and complete transparency.

Commitment

Our Promise

At iTOO, our promise lies not only in delivering world-class system Integration of products and services, but also in ensuring that we adhere to socially responsible business methods in all our commitments. We believe in fulfilling the social responsibilities by incorporating our core values, ‘integrity, trustworthiness and approachability’, in every projects, employee welfare programs, quality management procedures, health and safety issues and environmental activities.

Governance

Management Commitment

In all the concepts we implement with our contractual partners or subcontractors, regular checks across the relevant areas are done to examine the success of our management system and implemented measures.

iTOO strongly feels that occupational hazards and illnesses can be avoided with proper safety measures. Our motivated managers and efficient employees aid us to achieve our main goal to maintain an accident free workplace.

We aim to be an extension of your organisation, and to share our knowledge and technologies with you, our valued customer to help you achieve your goal of competing more effectively and profitably in today's competitive marketplace.

Values

Our Principles

Our company core value is driven by forging trusted relationships with our clients and partners. Through iTOO partnership and services, we strive to help our client's increase productivity, improve work efficiency, and see a return on their valuable investment.

  • Integrity

    Acting in good faith and establishing professional relationships based on transparency and ethics.

  • Professionalism

    Maintaining a proactive attitude with respect to ensuring the excellence of our efforts.

  • Respect

    Recognizing the value of people and their work, and of the social and natural environment in which we operate.

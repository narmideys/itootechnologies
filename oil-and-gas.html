Industries We Serve

Oil & Gas Sector

Some of the largest and most automated Oil & Gas companies struggle with transparency within their ecosystem of partners, suppliers and customers.

Let's Connect

Breaking Silos through transparency

While individual organizations and units within large energy conglomerates use conventional ERP systems to automate functions within their organizations — there lies a huge gap in the way information flows within the entire upstream and downstream value chain.

Our technology and expertise empower information exchange and governance of inter and intra organization workflows coherently and transparently.

Material Traceability

A Blockchain-powered platform used by Oil and Gas companies, manufacturers and stakeholders to manage material traceability from request through installation at the final location.

End-to-end real-time visibility enabled by DLT combats counterfeiting, digitizes material test certificates, and ensures authentic materials are delivered and cannot be forged or tampered with.

Custody / Transfer of Hydrocarbon

Oil & Gas conglomerates struggle to transparently streamline Custody/Royalty transfer of hydrocarbons to monitor and automate Meter-to-Cash processes — Metering, Ticketing, Verification, Billing, Invoicing and Collection.

Our Blockchain-powered platform enables transparent, efficient governance of these hydrocarbon supply-chain processes across stakeholders.

Oil Custody Chain

One of the critical elements of a successful hydrocarbon hub is transparency and traceability of product ownership throughout the product cycle.

Our Oil Custody Chain enables stakeholders involved in hydrocarbon transactions to access relevant information, maintain an audit trail of product ownership, and give financial institutions first-hand visibility of the underlying trade.

QAFI Platforms

Built for Qatar’s financial ecosystem

QAFi Consonance

Consonance is a state-of-the-art eKYC Platform that regulates and harmonizes national eKYC standards and data across all financial institutions within Qatar — banks, exchanges, insurers, and fintech companies.

Learn more about Consonance →

QAFi InstaChain IRMS

Insurance Recovery Management System for inter-recovery transactions. ITOO’s blockchain-based reconciliation & settlement platform helps organizations reconcile financial and non-financial transactions in real time through a trust-based network.

Learn more about InstaChain →

QAFi ASSURE

ITOO Bank Trust Network Platform (Workflow – Artefact Exchange) solves document exchange, verification and compliance within banks, customers and beneficiaries — bank guarantees, NOCs, demand drafts, statements and more.

Learn more about Assure →

QAFi Fragments

The next-gen end-to-end Real World Asset Tokenization and Investment platform. Fragments unlocks ownership through fractionalization — empowering asset owners with new funding routes and investors with exposure to unique assets.

Learn more about Fragments →

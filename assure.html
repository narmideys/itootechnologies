Bank Trust Network

ASSURE

A blockchain-based Bank Trust Network that enables secure verification and exchange of financial artifacts between institutions. It enhances transparency, trust, and compliance across banking ecosystems.

See Assure in Action Let's Connect

Demystifying The Future!

ASSURE – ITOO's Bank Trust Network solution, based on cutting-edge blockchain technology, is a next-gen solution to an age-old problem of document exchange, verification, and compliance within banks, its customers, and beneficiaries.

Whether it's a pyramid of trust between customer, issuing bank, and beneficiary bank—or a quadrant of trust between customer, beneficiary, and their respective banks—ASSURE is invented with foolproof features to seamlessly onboard participants.

These participants include customers, banks, central authorities, verifiers, and beneficiaries. The system can define more than 30+ types of documents such as bank guarantee, NOCs, DD, etc., encapsulating document-wise workflows.

  • The system has been designed to support multiple blockchain technologies from the backend and can integrate any ERP with its technology-agnostic Cipher orchestration middleware from the frontend.

  • ASSURE is the right fit for any group of banks or financial institutions looking towards near 6 sigma efficiency on document exchange operations and bringing an evolutionary change in customer experience and satisfaction.

What's inside Assure

  • Powered by Zero knowledge proof
  • Configurable smart contracts for 30+ document types
  • Inbuilt Artificial Intelligence to address de-duplication
  • Fast-track onboarding of banks using Cipher orchestration

Assure single handedly not just addresses the above stated challenges but also offers a mature ecosystem for an organization.

Assure Key Features

  1. Seamless Onboarding of Partner entities
  2. Proactive governance and monitoring of multiple blockchain networks
  3. A robust API framework that abstracts and integrates legacy systems with the blockchain plane
  4. Seamless deployment of smart contracts through intuitive User Interfaces

About Acuity

In 2017 ACUITY was initiated by an NHS clinician to support the vision: the right patient, the right doctor at the right time — providing a high-level view supporting command and control systems for individual patients or mass incidents.

Making it relevant to medical emergencies (e.g. stroke or heart attacks), mass trauma & terrorism, and epidemics (e.g. COVID-19, Ebola).

How Acuity Works

Dr. Asrar Rashid, ITOO's Chief Medical Consultant and Mr. Waqas Mirza, CEO of ITOO Technologies, explain how in a healthcare emergency, seconds can mean the difference between life and death. This is where ITOO's Acuity will help save lives.

In 2017 ITOO recognized the urgency of developing Acuity Super-Smart — merging blockchain and Artificial Intelligence with international clinicians and emergency care specialists to achieve the right patient at the right location with the right clinician.

Acuity App

Acuity works on an agile and phased concept which means that the software itself can be operational in a short time (circa 1 week) in order to save lives. Then the software capability is enhanced through deeper integration into the host system allowing for further sophistication. The ultimate is for clinicians to be interacting with one another, as the patient is en-route. In this way a management plan could be finalized prior to patient arrival at the hospital facility. Depending on the degree of data that is captured by Acuity, this can help drive data driven planning which could be automated through Artificial Intelligence principles.

Core Features of Acuity

Centralized Command & Control Center

Real-time hospital high-level view on asset availability, occupancy, and patient footfall.

Ambulance Tracking

Integrate into ambulance tracking to direct ambulances to the appropriate sites for treatment.

Insights of Facilities vs Patients Trends

Emergency centers across the city network as a coordinated country-wide unit via ERP and test-center integration.

Matching Demand with Supply

Facilitates matching clinical demand with the supply of healthcare capacity.

Monitoring individuals

Real-time patient footfall by integrating with ERP systems and IoT medical devices such as thermal scanning.

Major Incident & CCTV Coverage

Video footage of the site can be viewed by the relevant Authority for any man-made or natural major incident.

At a Glance

Preview of our city view system to understand how some of Acuity features work.

  • Real-time visibility of city-wide incidents, equipment availability across healthcare facilities, patient cases, ambulance tracking, and major incidents — retrieved by integrating with hospital ERP systems.
  • Algorithms optimizing ambulance flow based on weather, traffic, equipment & facility availability, relevant hospital specialists, and hospital queue time.
  • Ability to integrate with Civil Defense and Law Enforcement systems to capture and relay real-time details of major incidents, including site video footage for the Health Authority.
  • City-wide dashboards for governmental bodies and hospitals with drill-down to patient progress, treatments, and diagnosis.

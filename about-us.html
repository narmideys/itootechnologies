All About ITOO Technologies

At iTOO Technologies, we are committed to driving innovation and excellence by delivering next-generation digital solutions that empower businesses and governments to thrive in a rapidly evolving digital world.

iTOO stands for Innovating, Transforming, Optimizing Operations. As a 100% Qatari-owned company and the latest venture of a renowned technology group with over 25 years of industry leadership, iTOO has quickly become a trusted partner for enterprises, regulators, and governments across the GCC.

Our expertise spans Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence, Web 3.0, SaaS Solutions, and Smart City Projects, enabling organizations to stay ahead in today’s dynamic technology landscape.

QAFi

Powering Qatar’s Fintech Future

QAFi, a registered trademark of iTOO Technologies, represents Qatar Fintech Products — a suite of secure, innovative, and regulation-ready solutions designed to accelerate Qatar’s digital transformation. Aligned with Qatar’s financial vision, QAFi empowers banks, insurers, and enterprises with next-generation fintech capabilities.

SAGP Project Powered by COLLABREX

Implemented at SAGP (Sindh Agricultural Growth Project) within 4 months, Collabrex is streamlining the gap between farmers' demand and resources availability. With the help of Collabrex platform, Sindh Government is now able to reach out to farmers and meet their resources demand digitally in a robust and efficient manner.

4 mo SAGP implementation

Digital Marketplace for Farmers and Transparent Resource Tracking

Farmers have an access to a digital marketplace from where they can conveniently hire or lease relevant equipment and resources seamlessly – while the Sindh government can keep a complete and immutable trace of the leased / sold equipment and resource.

Empowering Farmers with Blockchain for Efficient Resource Access

Smart contracts are used to govern and automate a just and balanced disbursal of equipment to the most deserving farmers, while a complete trace and audit is available for all relevant parties to monitor.

Funded by the World Bank and deployed on Microsoft Azure, Collabrex is helping farmers in Sindh to increase their crops' yield manifolds by getting easy and timely access to resources provided by the Sindh government.

Collabrex is playing a key role in facilitating farmers by providing them a paperless experience in these challenging times of Covid-19. Farmers can avail Sindh government led facilities through digital channels without having to visit government offices.

The platform can be easily extended to support allocation, dispatch and tracking of non-farming related resources – including Medical Equipment to streamline distribution of Covid-19 related medical equipment and medicines.

Collabrex is a platform agnostic platform that can be replicated in any sector or vertical to help Government entities in distribution of resources in a just, transparent and efficient manner.
